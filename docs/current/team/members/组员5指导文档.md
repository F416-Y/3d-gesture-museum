### 11.4 AI 结构分析

先逐个零件分析：

    You are a 3D asset analyst for an indoor Chinese flower-and-bird art gallery.
    Analyze only the single object [PART NAME].
    Do not create a full building, courtyard, gate tower or landscape.
    Describe the geometry, assembly surfaces, proportions, material groups,
    front direction, origin placement and Blender cleanup steps.
    If the reference does not show a dimension, mark it as unknown.
    Do not invent exact measurements.

确认结构后再使用 Hyper3D。页面坐标不能由 AI 猜，必须以项目场景为准。

### 11.5 Hyper3D Prompt A：生模型

    Create a single isolated 3D model of a [PART NAME] for an indoor Jiangnan Chinese flower-and-bird gallery.
    Generate the raw structural form only, with correct proportions, clear assembly surfaces
    and a stable front direction.
    Target dimensions: [LENGTH] m long, [WIDTH] m wide, [HEIGHT] m high.
    Center the object at the origin and place its lowest point on Z=0.
    Include only the requested object: no full building, no courtyard, no landscape,
    no lake, no people, no plants, no camera and no lights.
    Use clean topology, moderate polygon count and a web-ready structure for GLB export.
    Preserve all visible panels, joints, openings and construction details from the reference.

### 11.6 Hyper3D Prompt B：材质

    Apply realistic materials to the existing [PART NAME]
    without changing its dimensions, silhouette, origin, orientation or assembly surfaces.
    Use restrained Jiangnan Chinese materials: warm aged timber, off-white plaster,
    pale gray masonry, dark roof trim and muted red-brown door accents where shown in the reference.
    Add subtle wood grain, controlled roughness variation, natural seams and light edge wear.
    Keep the result non-plastic and non-glossy, with no fantasy decoration,
    no excessive dirt, no bright colors and no modern signage.
    Do not add a room, courtyard, landscape, plants, people, camera or lights.
    Keep the material count and texture size suitable for real-time WebGL rendering.

### 11.7 Blender 清理步骤

每次只处理一个零件：

1. 删除相机、灯光、地面、背景和无关建筑。
2. 确认单位、竖直轴、正面和最低点。
3. 应用 Rotation 和 Scale。
4. 设置原点：柱/墙件放拼接基准点；门扇放铰链侧基准点；长案/书架放底面中心。
5. 清理破面、翻面、孤立点、重叠面和隐藏内面。
6. 检查门扇与门柱的间隙，门能关闭。
7. 检查后墙、墙柱和门顶栏的接缝，不能出现明显裂缝或穿插。
8. 检查长案、书架底面落地，不能挡住画作。
9. 检查纹理为相对路径，材质数量适合网页。
10. 旋转 360° 检查背面、底面和缺纹理。

导出到 assets/models/parts/opt/，文件名使用实际零件 ID。GLB 不能引用 C: 或 D: 盘绝对路径。

### 11.8 页面接入顺序

    back-wall → wall-pillar → door-column → door-leaf → door-canopy → long-table → bookshelf → 画作和植物

每接入一个零件后刷新页面，检查：

- 资源是否 200；
- 模型是否落地；
- 门柱、门扇和门顶栏是否对齐；
- 墙体是否无缝；
- 长案和书架是否挡画；
- 植物是否遮挡画作；
- 相机是否能看到主要展墙。

只修改 public/huaniao.html 和任务指定的模型引用，不改文创页面。

### 11.9 花鸟厅验收路线

1. 从花鸟厅入口进入。
2. 先正对主要挂画，检查画作无遮挡。
3. 左右转动，检查墙体、门柱、窗棂和植物。
4. 靠近门，确认门扇与门柱无穿插。
5. 走到长案和书架前，确认不挡画、不悬空。
6. 后退到中景，检查各零件接缝。
7. 退出花鸟厅，确认没有跳到序厅或文创厅。
